Upload imagery

Drag a GeoTIFF into its modality card, or choose a file. 32 MiB combined; at most 4 million band-pixels per TIFF.

Band order & metadata requirements

Optical: B01, B02, B03, B04, B05, B06, B07, B08, B8A, B09, B11, B12. SAR: VV, VH. Named descriptions take precedence. Acquisition dates use the acquisition_date TIFF tag (YYYY-MM-DD). Deep extraction requires 120 × 120 pixels.

Area of interest & dates Optional

AOI checks intersection. Features cover the full input patch; cropping is unavailable. Date filters require acquisition metadata.
